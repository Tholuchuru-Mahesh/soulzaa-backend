Soulzaaa Role Management & Functional Requirements
5. Moderator 
Overview
The Moderator is responsible for maintaining community safety, enforcing Soulzaaa Community Guidelines, reviewing reports, investigating violations, and taking moderation actions against users who violate platform policies.
Moderators are recommended by Officials, reviewed by Managers, and approved only by Admins.
Moderators work only within their assigned regions and during their assigned working shifts.
Moderators are responsible for maintaining community safety by reviewing reports, enforcing community guidelines, collecting system-generated evidence, and performing operational moderation within their assigned region.
Moderators are not responsible for platform operations, business decisions, financial approvals, or administrative management.
The Moderator is an anonymous internal role. Their identity must never be exposed to users or operational staff.

Moderator Profile

Examples

"A moderator removed this user from the room."
"This account has been suspended for violating platform policies."

 Note
The Moderator is an anonymous internal enforcement role responsible for maintaining community safety and enforcing Soulzaaa Community Guidelines. Moderators operate only within their assigned regions and working shifts, handling reports, investigating violations, and taking moderation actions according to platform policy.
Moderator identities must remain completely hidden from users and operational staff. They do not have public badges, profile frames, or public identifiers. Every moderation action automatically generates secure server-side evidence and permanent audit logs, ensuring complete transparency and accountability.
Moderators cannot perform administrative, financial, or platform management functions. They cannot use Invisible Mode, manually record sessions, or access investigation recordings. Their role is limited to moderation, documentation, recommendation, and enforcement within assigned permissions.
This architecture ensures fairness, accountability, operational transparency, Moderator safety, and protection against misuse while maintaining a secure and trusted Soulzaaa community.
